Book Months Ahead

Groups of 20+ book a median of about five months out—and Mardi Gras is the scarcest week of the year for villas that hold 14-30 people. If you're waiting, someone else is booking.

Do the Per-Person Math

On a normal weekend, 16 people splitting a villa works out to roughly $100-150 per person per night. Mardi Gras week prices higher—but splitting one villa still beats Mardi Gras hotel rates, and you don't need to fill every bed.
